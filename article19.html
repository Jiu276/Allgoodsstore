Bona Fide Masks: Exclusive US Distributor of Powecom KN95 Respirators

In an era where health and safety have become paramount, finding authentic, high-quality respiratory protection is more important than ever. Bona Fide Masks has emerged as a trusted name in personal protective equipment, serving as the exclusive distributor of Powecom KN95 respirators in the United States and Canada.

A Family Legacy of Trust Since 1938

The story of Bona Fide Masks begins long before the company's formation in 2020. The roots trace back to 1938, when the founders' great-grandfather and grandfather started Ball Chain Mfg. Co., Inc. in a modest garage behind their house in the Bronx, New York. This humble beginning marked the start of a family business that would span four generations and nearly a century of service.

Throughout its history, the family has demonstrated a commitment to helping others during times of need. During World War II, their newly-formed company pivoted to manufacture critical parts for the Thunderbolt fighter plane, contributing to the war effort. This tradition of service and adaptability would prove invaluable decades later.

Historical Milestones

  • 1938: Ball Chain Mfg. Co., Inc. founded in the Bronx, New York
  • 1940s: Manufactured parts for WWII Thunderbolt fighter planes
  • 2020: Formed Bona Fide Masks to address PPE shortage
  • Present: 4th generation family-owned business serving communities nationwide

Exclusive Distribution Partnership

In 2020, at the request of a local community leader during the height of the pandemic, Bona Fide Masks was formed to help address the critical shortage of personal protective equipment. The company quickly established itself as a reliable source for authentic respiratory protection.

Today, Bona Fide Masks proudly holds the distinction of being the exclusive distributor of Powecom KN95 products and Harley KN95 products in the United States and Canada. This exclusive partnership ensures that customers receive genuine, high-quality masks directly from authorized sources.

Exclusive Product Lines

  • Powecom KN95 Respirators: FDA-authorized masks (when EUA was active)
  • Harley KN95 Products: Premium respiratory protection
  • DemeTech Masks: Trusted US-manufactured masks

Note: Authentic Powecom and Harley KN95 masks are only available through Bonafidemasks.com

Authentic Quality You Can Trust

One of the biggest challenges in the PPE market has been ensuring authenticity. Counterfeit masks flooded the market during the pandemic, putting consumers at risk. Bona Fide Masks addresses this concern head-on with complete supply chain transparency.

Direct from Manufacturer

To strengthen supply chain security and promote the highest levels of transparency, Bona Fide Masks directs or handles all warehousing and distribution for their Powecom, DemeTech, and Harley masks. The company has dedicated experienced logistics professionals to this effort, ensuring that every mask purchased comes directly from the manufacturer without intermediaries.

FDA Authorization

Powecom masks were FDA-authorized under the Emergency Use Authorization (EUA) when it was active, demonstrating their commitment to meeting rigorous safety standards. This authorization provided additional assurance to healthcare workers and consumers during critical times.
